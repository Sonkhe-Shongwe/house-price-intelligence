## Design problem and reference research
Owner wants original, exceptional visual craft rather than a generic AI-generated frontend. Design around property evidence, not interchangeable dashboard cards: deliberate typography, asymmetric composition where useful, aligned data, selective emphasis, purposeful motion, meaningful whitespace, coherent icons and cartographic hierarchy. No decorative charts or unsupported data. Study [Linear's redesign](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui) and [2026 refresh](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh) for hierarchy, density and alignment; architectural editorial and Swiss modernist composition; [The Pudding](https://pudding.cool/) for data storytelling; [NN/g visual design principles](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/principles-visual-design/), [Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/), [Refactoring UI](https://www.refactoringui.com/) and [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/) as references, not templates or paid purchase commitments. [MapLibre styles](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-style-spec/) separate visual cartography from geometry and tiles. Road hierarchy, labels, water, backgrounds, subject and ranked markers must support investigation; no real-property assertions from virtual geometry.

## Signature experiences
1. Integrated property dossier with meaningful hierarchy and conditional map; clear evidence limitations.
2. Fast accessible address enquiry, candidate selection, no-match and ambiguity handling; no fictitious autocomplete.
3. Linked marker and evidence card with one selection state, no hover-driven camera jumps or silent rematching.
4. Comparison studio with aligned facts, exact monetary data, saved notes and simple reopening without UUID copying.
5. Evidence storytelling grounded in ranked actual evidence; no invented confidence or automatic winner.
