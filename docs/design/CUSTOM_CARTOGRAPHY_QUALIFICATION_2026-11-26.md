## Research and decision
[Maputnik](https://github.com/maplibre/maputnik) is a free visual editor for portable [MapLibre style JSON](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-style-spec/root/). Use it optionally as an authoring tool, not a runtime dependency. This change authors the JSON directly; an actual Maputnik import/export and designer usability test has not been performed. [Feature-state expressions](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-style-spec/expressions/) support selection paint without mutating geographic evidence. [Protomaps MapLibre integration](https://docs.protomaps.com/basemaps/maplibre), [flavors](https://docs.protomaps.com/basemaps/flavors) and [PMTiles protocol](https://docs.protomaps.com/pmtiles/maplibre) clarify that a full basemap needs separately governed tiles, fonts/sprites, attribution and delivery. No external tiles, provider, archive or geocoder acquired. Future regional spike requires source terms, size/Range/CORS/CSP/free hosting and label/zoom verification. No actual property coordinates or scale claims.
