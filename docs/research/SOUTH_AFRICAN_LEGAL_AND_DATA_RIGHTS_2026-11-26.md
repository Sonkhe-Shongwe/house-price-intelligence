## SA3.1 | Legal framework and practical controls

### 1. Four separate tests

1. Lawful access/disclosure by the data custodian, including statutory confidentiality, third-party rights and any access fees.
2. Lawful processing by the actual responsible party, purpose, basis, necessity, collection/notice, linkage and data-subject rights under POPIA.
3. Permission for each operation, including copying, joins, temporary analysis, durable retention, redacted output and any onward display or commercial use. Research permission is not a product licence.
4. Protected processing and responsible presentation in fact, not just a policy. Applicable owner approval and separate bounded empirical authorisation required. None of these tests can compensate for failure of another.

### 2. POPIA: Act 4 of 2013

Official [Act landing](https://www.gov.za/documents/protection-personal-information-act); [statutory text reproduced by LawLibrary, 26 Nov 2013](https://lawlibrary.org.za/akn/za/act/2013/4/eng@2013-11-26), sections identified below; [Regulator legal resources](https://inforegulator.org.za/legal/). Scope of the project may extend beyond the purely personal/household exclusion (s6); do not assume independent portfolio/noncommercial work exempt. Truly de-identified information meeting s6 and s1 definitions differs from pseudonymised records. A parcel reference is not necessarily a data-subject identifier; price/date/area/locality and other joinable attributes may nonetheless relate to an identifiable living person or applicable existing juristic person. Assess combinations, linkage and likely auxiliary sources, not names alone.

### 3. Linkage and hosting: specific escalation gates

- POPIA ss57–58 and [Regulator prior authorisation service](https://eservices.inforegulator.org.za/priorauthorisation/default.aspx), [revised guidance](https://inforegulator.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Revised-Guidance-Note-on-applications-for-Prior-Authorisation-adopted-by-Members-30-Jan.pdf) §3.3.1 (text, screenshot failed): unique identifiers **of data subjects**, repurposed from their original specific purpose **and** used to link information processed by other responsible parties, can trigger prior authorisation. SG21 is an identifier of property, not automatically a unique identifier of a data subject; determine actual key function and linkability. Do not equate every spatial join with s57, nor assume hashing avoids it. Obtain appropriate advice/regulator authorisation where required before processing; no experiment to determine applicability using real records. No credit reporting, criminal data, special personal information or children data in intended property pipeline. Consider applicable code of conduct/other triggers.
- s72: transfers of personal information to a foreign third party require a qualifying basis (including adequately protective law, binding rules/agreement or another statutory ground). Review exact actual host regions, subprocessors, support access, onward transfer, contracts and operator status; not a blanket requirement to host in SA, not blanket permission to use international cloud. No foreign real-data processing until approved protection and transfer conditions verified. Current Neon/Auth0/Drive/GitHub invented-data evidence does not establish SA suitability; no new provider access or change. Prefer local controlled processing where actually protected and permitted; no assumption a Windows machine is secure.
- Proposed role map: municipal/registry custodian determines its own disclosure purposes; HPI operator may be responsible party for independent purposes (actual identity/role to confirm); hosting/auth/backup services may be operators or independent parties depending on actual purpose/terms, not all interchangeable. Buyer personal notes/auth context separately governed. Maintain access inventory, written contracts, impact assessment, incident/correction process and source permission before intake.

### 4. PAIA: access mechanism, not a reuse licence

Official [Act landing](https://www.gov.za/nr/documents/promotion-access-information-act), [statutory text reproduced by LawLibrary, 30 Jun 2021](https://lawlibrary.org.za/akn/za/act/2000/2/eng@2021-06-30), [government Form 2 guidance](https://www.gov.za/services/services-residents/information-government/access-information), [Regulator fee structure](https://inforegulator.org.za/paia-fees-structure-2/). Public-body records: s11 entitlement subject to procedure/refusal grounds; s15 automatically available categories; s18 request, s22 fees, s25 decision, s28 severability, ss34/36/37 privacy/commercial/confidentiality protections and s46 narrow public-interest override, not a generic research exemption. Distinguish existing records from a new bespoke extract or analysis not yet held. Private-body s50 requires statutory conditions including rights-related need. No assertion any particular request must succeed. Request metadata/aggregate existing counts first, seek redacted existing export if lawfully releasable. Do not ask a municipality to violate upstream rights, seek owner identities, or invoke public interest unsupported.

Regulator published public-body request fee R100 and search/preparation charges R100/hour or part after first hour, capped at R300, plus possible copying/transmission or deposit; exemptions/own-record distinctions need actual applicability check. The regulator page distinguishes access-fee exemption; do not conflate it with blanket request fee waiver. R0 constraint makes ordinary paid PAIA route conditional/parked unless an expressly applicable no-cost path established; no claim of personal exemption or fee avoidance. Prioritise voluntary official research channel, automatically available materials or permitted open-data release. No Form 2 submitted, no deadlines or appeal started, no fee approval. A PAIA grant alone does not determine source licence, purpose compatibility or lawful downstream publication.

### 5. Municipal legislation: material confidentiality gate

[Municipal Property Rates Act 6/2004 government landing](https://www.gov.za/documents/local-government-municipal-property-rates-act-0), [2014 amendment landing](https://www.gov.za/ss/documents/local-government-municipal-property-rates-amendment-act-0), [consolidated statutory text on SAFLII](https://www.saflii.org/za/legis/consol_act/lgmpra2004454.pdf), ss41–50, 53 read as text; screenshot attempts failed. Ss41–42 provide specific inspection/information powers for valuers. **S44 limits disclosure of information obtained in exercising those powers, with enumerated exceptions connected to statutory duties/purpose, proceedings or court order.** This is a material new legal gate, not a blanket prohibition on all separately sourced municipal or public data. Determine field-by-field collection lineage and applicable lawful disclosure route; municipal/contractor consent alone cannot override statutory limitations. Do not solicit confidential valuation inspection information through contractor/employee workaround. A separate already-public or lawfully licensed source may be feasible, but no assumption of identical field rights.

Ss45–47 govern valuation (physical inspection not mandatory), s48 specifies roll particulars including property description, category, physical address, extent, assessed market value and owner as reasonably determinable; ss49–50 require notice/publication/inspection and allow fee-based extracts. Roll disclosure does not automatically extend to Template D sales files, inspection notes, internal building data or reusable bulk API. S53 concerns objection decisions/reasons, not a generic route to comparable data. No objection filed, no false owner status or misrepresentation. Municipal market value is not completed-sale consideration; HPI is not a statutory/professional valuation service. [Property Valuers Profession Act 47/2000] is the referenced professional framework in MPRA s39; confirm applicability separately if any estimation/appraisal scope proposed. Preserve evidence-only no valuation.

SA3.2: ask custodian whether each proposed field originated in s41/42 exercise, independently sourced registry data, permitted public roll or other lawful source, which disclosure authority applies, whether source contracts allow onward use and whether an appropriately minimised existing export/approved research arrangement can satisfy essential semantics. Do not rely on general open-data policy, Template D standard or research precedent to establish permission. Section 44 may foreclose specific building fields; if essential dated dwelling data cannot be lawfully obtained, stop/reassess source.

Do not treat proposed fair-use amendments as in-force permission; current post-judgment enactment status not conclusively established and no reliance on them. No copying proprietary datasets, hidden interfaces, terms circumvention or unapproved report exports.

Source attribution and transformation statements do not cure prohibited use; each dataset and source version must carry recorded permitted operations, conditions and upstream authority. If a source licence restricts disclosure or storage, enforce exact limits; a provider's marketing claims or public inspection afford no general licence. Consumer protection/financial and professional regulation review belongs to any later materially expanded scope; no credit, tax advice, valuation, discriminatory scoring or misleading accuracy claims. No assumption HPI is exempt by disclaimer.
