### Field/grain and acquisition design implications

| Essential semantic | National support documented | Unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sale transaction and exact ZAR cents/date | Registry price and registration data; eDRS digital access | Stable unique transaction ID, sale-event vs registration, eligible instrument, one-property allocation, repeat/non-market transfers, source availability dates; no proven R0 export. |
| Parcel identity and class | Registered descriptions; provincial schema SG_CODE 21 characters, erf and portion | Authoritative common key, historical parcel changes, dwelling/unit cardinality, full title vs sectional title. SG code is not transaction/building identity. |
| Dwelling area and dated building facts | No sufficient national open source identified; survey data land not building area | Need municipal valuer/qualified building source, measurement type and date, one eligible dwelling per comparable. Never substitute parcel area or footprint for living area. |
| Locality/geography | CSG, provincial metadata, NGI | Actual versioned coverage, transformation, precision, permitted use, street address-to-parcel selection, suburb/neighbourhood definitions. |
| Provenance and chronology | Official institutional and publication dates exist | Data observation/revision, actual availability at analysis cutoff, refresh/lag and correction policy; no point-in-time claim. |
| Market context | Published Stats SA aggregate index | Optional aggregate only; no transaction identity or microdata inference. |

### Material bridge to SA2.2: municipality-owned sales files

The public-facing [Umuziwabantu GV2027 valuation tender, Annexure D Template D](https://www.umuziwabantu.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/UMUZ112025-Valuation-roll.pdf), printed pp. 199–200, documents a required municipal sales file schema. Text inspected; screenshot calls for PDF indices 198–199 failed (cache miss). This is procurement specification evidence, not actual delivered rows, rights or a verified standard in every municipality. The tender itself carries a confidentiality footer; treat as documentation only and request permission from the custodian rather than redistributing it or any underlying dataset.

Material fields (paraphrased/selected schema names only): SG21/extended tenure key; locality and erf/portion; GBA in square metres; title deed references and up to several historical sale dates/prices; neighbourhood; multi-property sale indicator (MSP), sale inspection and validity indicators (SVI). Source template also includes identity/financing and free-text fields; do not request or acquire those. These documented fields are a promising alternative to assembling several disconnected national sources. Caveats: wide repeated history columns need normalisation, possible grouped parcels, no verified effective dates for GBA, validation for municipal valuation purpose not automatically market-sales suitability, no proven publication lag, source completeness, current availability, R0 access or onward reuse. Tender calls for delivery to municipality, not publication to the public. Do not presume municipality can sublicense registry-derived data. Verify exact sales-file availability, lineage and permitted projection with source owner if separately authorised. No owner names, addresses, values or property examples retrieved.

**Provisional route ranking:** municipal minimised sales/attributes extract with express permission (strongest local lead to investigate; Cape Town first, tender schema strengthens the broader route); CSG/provincial cadastral data as conditional supporting linkage; national Deeds/eDRS conventional route not R0 on documented tariff; alternative sanctioned registry/academic access unverified; Stats SA aggregate contextual only.
