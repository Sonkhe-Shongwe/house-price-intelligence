### Material bridge to SA2.2: municipality-owned sales files

The public-facing [Umuziwabantu GV2027 valuation tender, Annexure D Template D](https://www.umuziwabantu.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/UMUZ112025-Valuation-roll.pdf), printed pp. 199–200, documents a required municipal sales file schema. Text inspected; screenshot calls for PDF indices 198–199 failed (cache miss). This is procurement specification evidence, not actual delivered rows, rights or a verified standard in every municipality. The tender itself carries a confidentiality footer; treat as documentation only and request permission from the custodian rather than redistributing it or any underlying dataset.

Material fields (paraphrased/selected schema names only): SG21/extended tenure key; locality and erf/portion; GBA in square metres; title deed references and up to several historical sale dates/prices; neighbourhood; multi-property sale indicator (MSP), sale inspection and validity indicators (SVI). Source template also includes identity/financing and free-text fields; do not request or acquire those. These documented fields are a promising alternative to assembling several disconnected national sources. Caveats: wide repeated history columns need normalisation, possible grouped parcels, no verified effective dates for GBA, validation for municipal valuation purpose not automatically market-sales suitability, no proven publication lag, source completeness, current availability, R0 access or onward reuse. Tender calls for delivery to municipality, not publication to the public. Do not presume municipality can sublicense registry-derived data. Verify exact sales-file availability, lineage and permitted projection with source owner if separately authorised. No owner names, addresses, values or property examples retrieved.

**Provisional route ranking:** municipal minimised sales/attributes extract with express permission (strongest local lead to investigate; Cape Town first, tender schema strengthens the broader route); CSG/provincial cadastral data as conditional supporting linkage; national Deeds/eDRS conventional route not R0 on documented tariff; alternative sanctioned registry/academic access unverified; Stats SA aggregate contextual only. No final geography or source chosen.

**SA2.1 stop:** national source inventory, actual published conventional fee schedule, identifiers/grain/temporal/access gaps and legitimate alternative routes recorded, including material municipal sales-file lead. No empirical coverage established. Next SA2.2 municipal routes: compare Cape Town GV2025 nearby-sales and research/open-data request, municipal valuation sales-file specifications and official open datasets/metadata in Joburg, Tshwane, eThekwini, Ekurhuleni, Nelson Mandela Bay, Mbombela and evidence-led additional municipalities. ### Cross-municipality standard: material finding

The South African Council for the Property Valuers Profession [SAVS-02 Municipal Valuation Standard sMVPR v2021_2024.11b](https://www.sacpvp.co.za/standards/SAVS-02_Municipal%20Valuation%20Standard_sMVPR%20v2021_2024.11b.pdf), Template D following p.102, documents minimum sales-file reporting data and responsibility of the municipal valuer. It describes SG21 and extended 26-character tenure key, grouping/Unity of Use, sale-document history, characteristics, multi-property, inspection and sales-validity fields. Treat identifiers as strings, not numeric types. This is professional specification evidence, not verified legal mandate for public release or universal implementation. The already recorded Umuziwabantu GV2027 tender incorporates an earlier form of the template. A separate [eDumbe GV2025 procurement specification](https://edumbe.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/Main-Bid-Spec_Document_Valuation-roll-2024.pdf) pp.183, 192–200 (text excerpts, no row data) likewise specifies roll/GIS/property-type and sales files, audit history in the valuation system and current property extracts. This corroborates adoption in procurement, not delivery, completeness, source ownership or historical attribute availability. Source labels and versions must be reconciled with each custodian, not blindly treated as one nationwide physical schema.
