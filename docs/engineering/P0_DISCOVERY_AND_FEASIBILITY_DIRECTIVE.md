# P0 Discovery and Feasibility Directive

**Adopted:** 26 November 2026, owner requested documentation integration of the comprehensive P0 research, analysis, planning and implementation strategy. **Active delivery phase:** P0 only. **Active workstream and next task:** consult [checkpoint](../CHECKPOINT.md); initially P0-A.1. This is a subordinate execution directive, not a second roadmap, a new set of phase weights or a change to the [existing P0.1-P0.7 exit criteria](../P_PHASE_ROADMAP.md). The prior approved P0 estimate is 94% and overall 87.1%; both remain unchanged pending an evidence-backed milestone review. Method adoption is not product, market, rights or phase acceptance.

## 1. Purpose and gold-standard outcome

Establish that House Price Intelligence solves a meaningful homebuyer decision problem, has a defensible first market, viable data, permitted and ethical use, achievable technology, sustainable R0 operation, and an unambiguous testable MVP. P0 should hand P1 an approved strategic blueprint, not a finished backend, UI, deployment, commercial business or valuation model.

Eight assessment dimensions: product vision; target audience; market intelligence; data feasibility; legal/ethical feasibility; technical feasibility; financial feasibility; product scope and acceptance. These are discovery lenses, not independent weighted stages. The original real-property MVP and two-to-three-property shortlist remain governing; no synthetic-only substitute, feature removal or scope expansion by implication.

## 2. Research -> analysis -> planning -> implementation -> verification

For every workstream, first inspect existing evidence and explicitly identify the gap and the decision it affects. Research primary and official sources, peer-reviewed literature, credible surveys, actual reproducible experiments and official competitor documentation where relevant. Supplement with consumer experiences, clearly labelled as anecdotal; do not claim actual user validation from desk research or invent interview subjects. Record source title, URL, date, method/sample/geography, finding, limitations, relevance, conflicting evidence and resulting implication. Separate observation, inference, hypothesis and owner decision. Prefer the strongest relevant evidence, not source volume or artificial research quotas.

Analyse alternatives, bias, trade-offs and disconfirming evidence; planning converts findings to requirements, scope, risk decisions and acceptance criteria. Implementation in P0 means proportionate documents/specifications or indispensable safe feasibility experiments, not ordinary application feature engineering. Verify against the pre-defined workstream stopping criterion, preserve exact evidence, identify unresolved blockers and record next action in the checkpoint. Apply the existing [eight-step lean lifecycle](LEAN_ENGINEERING_DIRECTIVE.md) proportionately. Reuse existing reports; only create a new document if no existing canonical home is adequate. Do not perform new live source collection, recruit/contact users or publishers, create accounts/resources, deploy or change credentials without the relevant approval. No personal/real property records, credentials or raw extracts in Git.
