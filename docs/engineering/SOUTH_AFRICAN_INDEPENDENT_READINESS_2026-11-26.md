Cannot run the original retrospective cohort unchanged or infer a complete fixed-window ledger from it. A separately approved current snapshot investigation would need actual current entry, freshness and declared subset limitations. No such scope accepted.
- Valuation-selected transactions versus registry population: record screening and scope, whether eligible events may be excluded and why. Report conditional coverage only for the declared source cohort; not universal market coverage. No empirical coverage yet.

Proposed temporal contract: preserve agreement/sale date, registration date, attribute observation/effective interval, first evidenced availability and source extraction/revision independently; unknown stays unknown. In a current-source retrospective replay use only source-qualified event dates strictly earlier than subject cutoff and attribute versions demonstrably applicable to the property at the candidate sale and subject analysis cutoff, respectively; report backfill/availability unknown and **not** historical-as-of verified. If the date of observation itself is after the relevant cutoff, do not assume earlier physical state merely from an effective date; require documented historical applicability, explicit provenance and no leakage claim. For genuinely contemporaneous/as-of qualified claims require actual evidence that every input used was available by analysis cutoff, not registration date alone. If unavailable then exclude from as-of selection; if unknown then no as-of claim. Subject held-out sale/price is not a ranking input. Date ordering contradictions (including impossible source dates) fail rather than silently impute; custodian definitions determine legitimate exceptions/corrections. Proposed 365-day earlier window, 3–5 distinct other properties, same locality, ±25% like-defined area remain research starting points only, not accepted local thresholds or guarantees.
