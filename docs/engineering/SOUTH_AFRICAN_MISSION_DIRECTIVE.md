## 1. Outcome, boundaries and recovery

Deliver a credible, South African-first residential property intelligence product for an individual comparing two or three homes in a supported market: Investigate / Compare / Decide. The primary objective is real, relevant, traceable completed-sale evidence; not an overseas dataset converted to rand, fictional-only replacement, listing aggregator, municipal valuation masquerading as sale, generic property portal or automated appraisal. A single locality/class is the initial qualification target, not a national coverage claim. Local buyer features and alternative technologies are open to evidence-led reconsideration. Preserve the accepted [foundation](../reviews/P0_A_PRODUCT_FOUNDATION_REVIEW_2026-11-26.md), [product brief](../product/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md), [principles/measures](../product/PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES_AND_SUCCESS_2026-11-26.md) and [competitive research](../product/COMPETITIVE_INTELLIGENCE_2026-11-26.md); refine with local evidence, obtain applicable approvals for material changes. No implied demand, customer recruitment or final specification.

## 5. Decision gates and outcome logic

**GO for local direction** requires a documented permitted and sustainable R0 acquisition/display path, trustworthy grain and identity, actual bounded relevant earlier-sales and contemporary entry/availability evidence adequate for the accepted buyer job, defensible source versions and limitations, protected handling and accepted scope. An evidence-only retrospective demo is not proof of contemporary buyer usefulness; an alternative narrower local product needs explicit scope approval. Actual demand/incremental benefit remains separate M1-M8 validation; no invented success.

**BLOCKED** if credible route needs indispensable response, ethics approval, fee exception, source permission or new scoped empirical approval, or protection not qualified. Prepare one concrete request/decision with exact fields, locality, dates, uses, controls, cost ceiling and fallback. Do not send or assent without approval, do not use unsupported data while waiting. Resume only safe bounded investigation where justified, otherwise report blocker.

**NO-GO** if serious evidenced alternatives cannot meet essential lawful R0 data/identity/time/coverage requirements, or no appropriate buyer proposition remains under approved scope. Document failed route vs unknown; stop without wasting effort on implementation; return to saved P0-C supported-boundary review and integrate SA evidence. International fallback is genuine, not disguised as SA. Escalate explicit material change/rebaseline decision only when evidence justifies it.

**SA0 stopping criterion:** owner instruction recorded; dedicated sequential mission strategy, return point, noncompensatory gates, conditional implementation/technology plan and first credible source-route evidence documented. **Next SA1 national property ecosystem and customer/competitor research**; then SA2 in bounded source-family slices, SA3 legal review and SA4 only after prerequisites. No new data or contact, no spend, no implementation migration or score change. All full phase gates remain open.
