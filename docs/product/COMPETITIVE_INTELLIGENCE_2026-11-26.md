## B.1 | Initial consumer-platform benchmark

Purpose: establish direct overlap with the existing US shortlist decision, not rank platforms or infer missing features. Select Zillow and Redfin because their documented US buyer workflows overlap with discovery, historical evidence and saved homes; earlier [UX research](../design/UX_RESEARCH_AND_PRODUCT_DIRECTION_2026-11-23.md) already identified Redfin as a reference. This is deliberately a two-platform baseline, not a comprehensive market inventory. B.2 will examine additional relevant portals, specialist tools and non-software alternatives before consolidated comparison. All sources checked 26 November 2026; vendor documentation, not independent usage or accuracy experiments. US-oriented, feature availability can vary by account, platform and geography. Publication dates are not assumed when unavailable.

### Initial job comparison

Documented means the source explicitly describes the capability. Not established means the reviewed documentation does not establish that exact behaviour, not that the platform lacks it. HPI entries below are accepted intended scope; actual real-data delivery and buyer value remain unvalidated.

| Job | Zillow | Redfin | HPI intended |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| JT1 identify subject | Property search and home detail | Search and home detail | Bounded explicit parcel selection, abstain unresolved |
| JT2 historical evidence | Sale/price history and sold-home guidance; separate Zestimate | Nearby sold homes and model-adjusted owner comparables | Earlier eligible completed sales and selection rationale, not current estimate |
| JT3 evidence limitations | Estimate is not appraisal; insufficient data may suppress estimate | Adjusted estimates differentiated from reported sales; owner workflow restricted | Explicit four evidence states, date/scope limitations and no valuation |
| JT4 compare shortlisted homes | Publisher documented side-by-side saved-home comparison | Favourites/lists and comments; exact side-by-side evidence workflow not established | Two-to-three-home aligned evidence comparison |
| JT5 preserve research | Saved homes and updates; exact immutable evidence reopen not established | Account lists and updates; exact immutable evidence reopen not established | Verified frozen evidence and notes, subject to authorised retention |
| JT6 next action | Discovery, research and guidance | Research, professional agent handoff | Buyer-owned next questions and appropriate abstention |

Counterevidence: Zillow already offers comparison and both established platforms provide historical context and saved homes. Zillow also documents withholding estimates where data is inadequate. Redfin supports owner-selected comps and discloses adjusted price semantics. No claim that comparison, explainability, limitations or saving alone is unique. HPI lacks licensed current listings and validated current estimates; these are real limitations for a contemporary decision. Potential value of a traceable, frozen historical evidence comparison independent of valuation is a hypothesis, not verified unique functionality or superiority. Agents/portals may suffice. No vendor records copied; no inference of HPI rights from competitor capabilities.
