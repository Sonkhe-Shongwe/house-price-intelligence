**Counterevidence and resulting design implications (analysis, not new approved requirements).** Comparison, notes, maps, property history, filtering, retained reports and explicit comp reasoning are not defensible uniqueness claims. Cook Viewer makes a same-market official workflow a mandatory comparison reference; RPR and agents make richer current/contextual analysis credible alternatives. HPI's narrower opportunity is the combination of self-directed, consistent earlier-sale evidence across two or three candidate properties, transparent selection/abstention and recoverable exact evidence with dates and rule versions, if real data rights, scope and usability can be established. A manual worksheet can reproduce much of this in principle; convenience, comprehension and time savings remain unmeasured. No competitor absence or superiority inferred from incomplete documentation. Keep current two-to-three-property scope and existing evidence statuses, do not add valuation, AI, live listings, professional CMA, collaboration, or export merely for parity. R0 applies to our research and implementation; the documented member benefit is not free unrestricted public access.

### Strategic trade-offs and rejected claims

| Option | Disposition and rationale |
| --- | --- |
| General listing-discovery marketplace | Reject for current flagship: incumbent strengths, licensed inventory/freshness and operating burden; no demonstrated need or R0 path. |
| Automated valuation product | Conditional post-MVP only: competition already estimates values; current retrospective errors, time-availability limitations and rights cannot support a current valuation. Independent holdout and owner decision mandatory. |
| Professional appraisal/CMA or agent replacement | Reject current target shift: richer professional data and contextual judgement, distinct role and legal/accuracy obligations; no evidence to justify. |
| Generic feature parity (AI assistant, alerts, messaging, collaboration, exports, more properties) | Reject as default response to competitor features. Evaluate only if required by evidence and separately accepted scope where applicable. |
| Buyer-controlled historical investigation and shortlist | Retain as provisional candidate, not immutable design. Distinct workflow combination is plausible; uniqueness, usefulness, supported geography, feasibility and safe retention remain open. |

Claim discipline: no 'only', 'first', 'better than Zillow', 'more accurate than agents', 'unbiased', 'current fair price' or 'proven savings'. Describe intended behaviour precisely: traceable earlier recorded-sale evidence, explicitly bounded applicability, and consistent buyer comparison/reopen where permitted. No claim that competitors lack an equivalent workflow. Current architecture and user experience are assets, not constraints: research can justify redesign, data/market substitution, refactoring or replacement; compare alternatives against buyer outcomes, correctness, costs, rights and migration risks. No migration authorised by this comparison.
