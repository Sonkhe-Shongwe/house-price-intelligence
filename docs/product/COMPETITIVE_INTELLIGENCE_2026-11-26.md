Counterevidence: Zillow already offers comparison and both established platforms provide historical context and saved homes. Zillow also documents withholding estimates where data is inadequate. Redfin supports owner-selected comps and discloses adjusted price semantics. No claim that comparison, explainability, limitations or saving alone is unique. HPI lacks licensed current listings and validated current estimates; these are real limitations for a contemporary decision. Potential value of a traceable, frozen historical evidence comparison independent of valuation is a hypothesis, not verified unique functionality or superiority. Agents/portals may suffice. **Counterevidence and resulting design implications (analysis, not new approved requirements).** Comparison, notes, maps, property history, filtering, retained reports and explicit comp reasoning are not defensible uniqueness claims. Cook Viewer makes a same-market official workflow a mandatory comparison reference; RPR and agents make richer current/contextual analysis credible alternatives. HPI's narrower opportunity is the combination of self-directed, consistent earlier-sale evidence across two or three candidate properties, transparent selection/abstention and recoverable exact evidence with dates and rule versions, if real data rights, scope and usability can be established. A manual worksheet can reproduce much of this in principle; convenience, comprehension and time savings remain unmeasured. No competitor absence or superiority inferred from incomplete documentation. Keep current two-to-three-property scope and existing evidence statuses, do not add valuation, AI, live listings, professional CMA, collaboration, or export merely for parity. R0 applies to our research and implementation; the documented member benefit is not free unrestricted public access.
