## 1. Product principles

The following synthesise existing obligations and proposed design priorities, not an amendment to accepted scope.

| Principle | Operational meaning | Violation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence before prediction | Eligible earlier recorded sales and permitted attributes precede any inference; valuation absent for evidence-only release | Unsupported estimate or price verdict |
| Explainability before complexity | Show selection rationale, meaningful differences, date, source scope and evidence tier using permitted fields | Unexplained conclusion, false claim of comparable equivalence |
| Integrity before convenience | Verified frozen data, exact monetary units, identity boundaries, authorised access and source-use/retention safeguards | Incorrect price, wrong subject, disclosure, altered evidence or unauthorised reuse |
| Transparency before precision | Distinguish asking price, reported completed consideration, exploratory fallback, insufficiency and outside coverage; acknowledge publication lag | Presenting historical evidence as current, missing as zero or fallback as validated valuation |
| Customer autonomy | Buyer chooses next action, including pause and external professional advice | Prescriptive offer/purchase, forced winner, pressure or claim of guaranteed savings |
| Product before technology | Select simplest qualified implementation for customer and evidence needs; reuse existing verified work | Unjustified model, tool or architecture solely for novelty |
| Focus before expansion | One qualified local market/class, two or three distinct homes; preserve agreed non-goals and R0 | New data/markets/features or spending without applicable evidence and approval |
| Accessibility by design | Understandable language, keyboard access, responsive core workflow and usable error/recovery states | Core task inaccessible or caveats hidden; no blanket compliance claim from partial tests |

## 2. North Star and proposed formative targets

**North Star: evidence-informed decision task success.** A participant correctly identifies the intended enquiry or its unresolved status, distinguishes asking and completed prices, explains relevant evidence and limitations, compares distinct alternatives, and states an appropriate next action without critical misconception or moderator rescue. Correct abstention is success when the scenario warrants it; buying a property is not the outcome.

Proposed formative evaluation targets are design choices for owner review, not industry benchmarks or achieved values. For a planned small exploratory study, use at least 80% unassisted success on each applicable job and the composite, with zero observed critical misconceptions on required safety probes. The 80% threshold is a pragmatic initial usability floor, not a statistical population estimate or release permission. The zero-observed threshold is a stop-and-review rule, not proof of zero population risk. Small samples require reporting raw counts and uncertainty, never rounding a failure into a pass.

| Metric / jobs | Numerator / denominator | Proposed formative target and method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1 Subject and coverage comprehension (JT1/VP1) | Participants correctly resolve or recognise unresolved identity and coverage in all assigned identity probes / participants assigned those probes | >=80%; observe explicit selection or refusal, ask parcel vs building distinction |
| M2 Price and comparable comprehension (JT2/VP2) | Participants correctly distinguish buyer-reported asking price vs recorded sale, explain one matching reason, material difference and date in all assigned applicable probes / participants assigned those probes | >=80%; use predefined scenario answer key; no credit for unsupported valuation inference |
| M3 Limitations and abstention (JT3/VP3) | Participants correctly interpret all four evidence states and reject stale evidence as a current-price conclusion / participants assigned limitation probes | >=80%; zero observed critical errors; probe supported, exploratory, insufficient, outside coverage and retrospective timing |
| M4 Consistent comparison (JT4/VP4) | Participants compare at least two distinct homes, identify two predefined material differences and uneven evidence without forced ranking / participants assigned comparison | >=80%; compare against scenario answer key, with a supported/unsupported pair included |
| M5 Research continuity (JT5/VP5) | Participants save and reopen the assigned two-home comparison, recover original evidence/date and a note, identify that it was not refreshed / participants assigned authorised reopen task | >=80%; use qualified fictional mode first; no claim of unqualified private edit/recovery |
| M6 Next-action quality (JT6/VP6) | Participants give a reasoned next action consistent with evidence and identify an unresolved question or appropriate handoff / participants assigned decision task | >=80%; no credit for treating HPI as appraisal or offer instruction |
| M7 Composite North Star | Participants passing every applicable M1-M6 job without critical misconception or assistance / participants assigned complete task suite | >=80%; participants rather than attempts counted once, all required scenarios assigned; legitimate abstention gets credit |
| M8 Incremental value (VP2-VP6) | Paired difference in composite and job success counts/percentages, critical errors, omissions and total active task time (including entry/learning/reopen) versus current-workflow comparison; denominator = participants assigned both conditions, paired complete count also reported | Exploratory positive signal: composite success higher, or equal success with median paired active time lower, without more critical errors or materially worse job accuracy; do not claim superiority from a small sample |

M1-M7 report x/n, exact proportion, task-level failures and reasons; a target passes only if x >= ceil(0.8*n), n>0, all required cases assigned and safety gate satisfied. Noncompletion, abandoned tasks, missing answers and moderator rescue count as failure for assigned applicable tasks; no silent denominator exclusions. Missing comparator pairs cannot establish M8; report missingness, complete pairs and assigned-condition failures separately, no selective comparison. No automated behavioural telemetry or new analytics infrastructure required. Time and omissions are diagnostics, not grounds to trade away safety. JT7/JT8 confidence and communication can be recorded as exploratory self-report/observed explanation, never substitute for accuracy. No NPS, conversion, financial savings, price accuracy or adoption target is asserted without suitable evidence.

## 3. Existing mandatory technical / data guardrails

These are pass/fail obligations, not averaged customer metrics or new phase gates. Verify the existing applicable acceptance contracts in their stated mode. For every tested or audited relevant case, require full compliance; one failure blocks the relevant claim until reviewed and corrected. A zero denominator is not a pass.

| Guard | Required behaviour and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| G1 Identity and data grain | Distinguish transaction, parcel, building; no guessed ambiguous address, unsupported class/geography or physical building verification claim. Audit coverage, record counts, joins and property-entry boundary under P0-C/P1; P0.4 open. |
| G2 Comparable integrity | Every displayed comparable internally links to one eligible earlier sale and correct permitted attributes/price/date/source/version; exclusions, duplicates, future/same-day, multi-parcel, subject sale and policy tests per existing contracts. Exact cents; prior-year join is proxy only. No raw comparable ID disclosure required. |
| G3 Truthful evidence state | Correct insufficiency/out-of-coverage, clear exploratory fallback, dates and absence of valuation; no imputation or fabricated comparison. Test known positive and negative scenarios; compare all assigned status cases, not only those that produce results. |
| G4 Frozen continuity | Verified two-to-three distinct-home save/reopen with evidence unchanged, permitted notes and recovery; reject tamper, unauthorised, expired or inconsistent records, no silent rematch. Existing [P3](../engineering/P3_APPLICATION_CONTRACT.md), [P6](../engineering/P6_SYNTHETIC_SHORTLIST_CONTRACT.md) and [workspace](../engineering/BUYER_WORKSPACE_UX_V1.md) define fictional contract. Actual combined private lifecycle acceptance still open. |
| G5 Rights, privacy and protection | No unauthorised real intake, public raw data, credentials, restricted identifiers, owner/contact data or geometry. Narrow permitted projection, authorised retention maximum 30 days from intake, access/integrity failure closed, confidential recovery, existing R0 boundary. [Private beta](../engineering/PRIVATE_REAL_DATA_BETA_V1.md) and [source register](../research/SOURCE_REGISTER.md) govern; P0-D/E/G pending. |
| G6 Usability and reliability | Existing keyboard/reflow, explicit error states, regression/CI and release contracts; no claim of full accessibility, provider or production readiness solely from CI. Review actual intended mode. |
| G7 Conditional estimation | No numeric valuation in evidence-only release. If later justified, existing product brief requires chronological held-out comparison with baseline, accuracy and interval coverage/width; no relaxation or unvalidated interval. |

Prior [FR qualification](../engineering/FULL_RELEASE_QUALIFICATION_V1.md) canonical corrected holdout: 142 eligible sales (83/59), 59 later subjects with selected comparables (41 neighbourhood, 18 exploratory); paired MAE improvement 16.8% versus township baseline, absolute MAE $152,615.81 and MdAPE 22.53%, weak fallback. Retrospective current-source evaluation, not publication-time as-of proof. These are measured historical research figures, **not** buyer-task success or valuation acceptance. Do not use 59/59 coverage as evidence of current address support or demand. Current 2024-2025 / 2023-2024 data scope is candidate only; P0-C must resolve relevance/freshness and supported identity without re-extraction in A.9. If the intended purchase needs current evidence that cannot be supplied, the product is not yet viable for that use case even with excellent interface metrics.

## 4. Proposed validation protocol (not executed)

1. Agree intended buyer scenario, scope, metric definitions and rubric with owner. Confirm safety/rights requirements independently. No recruitment or contact approved; begin with internal synthetic walkthrough when authorised in relevant phase, not a substitute for buyer study.
2. Freeze two matched fictional scenario sets (or separately authorised real scenarios) with same decision information and comparable difficulty; include a supported case, exploratory spatial, insufficient/out-of-coverage, ambiguity and stale/retrospective cases; include reopen after permitted interruption. Never use prohibited real records.
3. For an approved exploratory buyer study propose 6-8 distinct prospective participants across first-time/occasional and repeat/unfamiliar-market lenses, not a representative sample or new recruitment commitment. Set final sample/consent/privacy rules before recruitment. For small n use descriptive counts and report limitations; an incomplete or unbalanced study cannot establish population demand.
4. Ask about actual prior shortlist process and alternatives before demonstrating HPI. Compare current method versus HPI using equivalent evidence sets and counterbalanced order where feasible. Record need, usability, adoption intention and data availability separately; do not prime benefits. Use same time rules and answer-key rubric; record unassisted first attempt and any rescue.
5. Score participant-level M1-M7 using predetermined scenario-specific correct answers (including legitimate abstention); record raw counts, missingness, critical errors, noncritical errors, task time and omissions. M8 paired outcomes only where comparable, report attrition. No post-hoc target tuning; report order/learning effects, sample selection and uncertainty.
6. Independently audit G1-G7 in applicable environment, preserve verified historical technical evidence and rerun only required tests when implementation changes. No source rows, real provider activation or new instrumentation for A.9.
7. Review results with owner. A critical misconception (wrong parcel treated as verified, asking price treated as sale, stale as current, insufficient/fallback as valuation, inferred HPI buy/offer instruction) or technical/data protection breach triggers stop/review and correction before any affected claim. If M1-M7 fail, investigate failure rather than lower threshold; if M8 shows no incremental benefit, narrow/reject proposition rather than expand automatically. A favourable fictional task study cannot substitute for real feasibility, actual demand or approval. All outcomes and thresholds remain proposed; no testing or measured results in A.9.

## 5. Three separate evidence levels and acceptance boundary

- Technical qualification: existing synthetic and actual invented-data CI/provider proof with remaining gates; tests are not human validation.
- Customer value: task outcomes and incremental benefit as above; no participant study run, no evidence of adoption or market fit.
- Real-world readiness: authorised source scope, identity, data coverage and freshness, rights, protected storage, final market and release decision. P0.4-P0.7 remain open; neither technical nor formative success can waive these.

A.10 is a foundation review and applicable owner acceptance of the proposed audience, jobs, value, positioning and principles/measurement plan, with unresolved hypotheses recorded. It is not a claim that human targets have been attained; the actual consumer/real-world validation remains pending under appropriate later approvals. Do not add metrics as new P0 stage weights or require a new paid study to close a desk-research task. Original P0 exit criteria govern.
