### Property type, tenure and identity

| Category | Key distinction and candidate data requirement | Scope implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Freehold single-dwelling house | Erf/portion, township and registered description plus source-specific cadastral key; land vs dwelling m2, independently established single-property sale/physical building facts. | Candidate first class only; no blanket one-building or one-owner assumption from parcel. |
| Sectional title | Section and scheme identity, common property and applicable levies; unit size not whole erf/complex area. | Separate grain/identity and comparable policy; no naive erf-price assignment. |
| Estate/gated community | May contain freehold or sectional-title properties; estate is context, not a tenure type. HOA/body corporate obligations and boundaries may differ. | Compare like with like; not automatically include all estate properties or assign costs. |
| Vacant land, agriculture, commercial/mixed-use, informal/nonregistered tenure | Different evidence, ownership, rights and buyer jobs; dwellings and transfers not interchangeable. | Outside initial candidate workflow unless separately qualified; excluded does not mean socially unimportant. Formal registry cohort does not represent all housing. |

Official [Sectional Titles Act overview](https://www.gov.za/documents/sectional-titles-act-17-sep-1986-0000) describes separate sections and shared property; [CSOS FAQ](https://csos.org.za/faqs/) discusses scheme governance and contributions. Government Deeds information specifies registered descriptions rather than street address and scheme/section identification. It also describes owner details for lookup; do not request or collect owner/ID details for HPI. Need a permitted property-first resolution route under SA2/SA3, not owner-based search. [Municipal Property Rates Act overview](https://www.gov.za/documents/local-government-municipal-property-rates-act-0), [COGTA rates explanation](https://www.cogta.gov.za/index.php/2016/08/30/what-are-municipal-property-rates/) and [Cape Town valuation timing](https://www.capetown.gov.za/family-and-home/residential-property-and-houses/property-valuations/current-and-upcoming-valuations/) / [eThekwini roll](https://www.durban.gov.za/page/valuation-roll) support municipality/date specificity; last two full fetch failed, search excerpts only. Do not treat municipal assessed market value as transaction price. Estate-specific context is a design inference supported by [Lightstone help](https://www.lightstone.co.za/AVM/content/help/default.aspx) describing estate/scheme reports; no measured effect on price.

### Price and time dictionary (requirements, not a new schema)

- Asking price: buyer observation of advertised amount at stated date; not a registered sale or appraisal. Accepted/negotiated amount: agreement consideration, possibly pending registration; do not use as completed-sale comparable.
- Registered sale consideration: publisher's stated transaction value with documented event/registration dates and eligibility; excludes non-market/ambiguous transfers under an eventual verified policy. ZAR exact cents. Registration may lag agreement; source ingestion/publication may lag both.
- Municipal valuation: assessed amount for a dated valuation roll and rating category. Not transaction evidence; municipal rates use applicable rate-in-rand, rebates and policy. No fixed national rate or valuation-to-sale conversion.
- Bank/appraiser estimate or AVM: distinct estimated amount with methodology/time; not recorded sale, not approved HPI output. Bond principal is funding, not price. Aggregate indices/trends are context, not individual price evidence.
- Transfer duty/VAT: different possible tax treatment; [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/) and [transfer duty guide](https://www.sars.gov.za/guide-for-transfer-duty-via-efiling/) distinguish treatment and acquisition dates. No calculator, thresholds or personal tax guidance adopted. If later scoped, version rules by effective date and verify with relevant authorities. Separate one-time transaction costs (transfer/conveyancing, applicable tax, bond fees) from recurring rates, levies, insurance and maintenance, plus finance costs conditional on financing.

**Proposed primary segment:** individual prospective owner-occupier already considering two or three homes in a demonstrably supported SA locality/class, researching asking prices before making or finalising an offer. First-time/mortgage-dependent and unfamiliar-locality buyers are plausible overlapping high-need groups; repeat/cash buyers remain possible, no demographic or income exclusion. Do not collect personal finance data, proxy protected traits, create a new mandatory first-time-only persona, promise affordability or claim nationwide representativeness. The wider housing ecosystem includes informal/nonregistered housing, subsidised routes and rental households; formal resale data cannot represent their decisions. Property selection remains data-first and SA preferred, not a chosen pilot city or price band.

## 5. SA product scope implications and handoff

**Retain as core:** Investigate / Compare / Decide, explicit property identity/coverage, user-reported asking price vs recorded sale cents and dates, qualified earlier evidence, separate limitations/abstention, two-to-three-home comparison, frozen authorised evidence and notes, next questions. Localise terminology ZAR, m2, erf/portion, township vs suburb/municipality, sectional scheme/unit if qualified, registration vs sale/valuation dates; no local records or schema adopted. Candidate freehold single-dwelling class and one local geography only, Cape Town source lead first but no final choice.

**Evaluate later, not must-have:** transfer-cost/rates/levies context, aggregate market trends, planning/spatial context, accessibility/low-bandwidth and language options where user evidence supports them. Avoid crime/safety rankings, demographic steering, unauthorised personal records and false precision. Financial calculators require versioned official rules, assumptions and no credit/tax advice; no automatic scope expansion. No listings portal, agent replacement, valuation model, direct financing, ownership certification or national market promise. SA5/SA6 require explicit material scope/architecture decisions; do not redesign during SA1.

**SA1 stopping criterion:** local lifecycle/actors, price and property-type distinctions, provisional buyer, local secondary evidence, nine documented/analysed alternatives vs JT1-JT6, counterevidence and four falsifiable hypotheses tied to existing measures, scope implications documented. Bounded desk research complete, not customer demand, market qualification or approved new features. Next **SA2.1 national official acquisition routes and field/grain assessment**: Deeds Registry/DeedsWeb, Chief Surveyor-General, Stats SA and relevant official national catalogue/documentation, assess exact access mode/cost, identifiers, required fields, temporal coverage, restrictions and permitted alternative paths; no property lookups/downloads/accounts/contacts. Then municipal (Cape Town first), academic/controlled/commercial and alternative routes, consolidated source viability review; SA3 rights thereafter. Preserve international return point and all protections. No expenditure, app edits or phase credit; P0 94%, weighted MVP 87.1%, R0.
