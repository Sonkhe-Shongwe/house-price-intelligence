## 4. Candidate value proposition

For prospective owner-occupiers comparing two or three already shortlisted homes in a demonstrably supported local market and property class, House Price Intelligence is an evidence-based property decision workspace designed to organise relevant historical completed sales, explain why they are comparable, expose evidence limitations and preserve the buyer's own comparison and questions. It is intended to complement existing research and professional advice, not supply a current valuation, live inventory or purchase recommendation.

Short expression: **Understand the evidence. Compare the alternatives. Decide your next step.**

Existing product identity remains **Investigate / Compare / Decide**. The short expression is a proposed description of value, not a rebrand, final positioning, new release promise or commercial advertisement. A.8 will reconcile vision/positioning; A.10 obtains applicable approval.

## 6. Benefit validation and rejection rules

Before claims of added value, compare the buyer's current process with the same bounded decision task using HPI and equivalent available information, with clearly fictional or expressly authorised scenarios and permission for any participant involvement. Separate need from usability, and current evidence availability from interface effects. Observe JT1-JT6 outcomes, correctness of price/coverage interpretations, recognition of insufficient evidence, omissions, effort, frozen reopen and appropriate next questions. Include well-supported, exploratory, insufficient and stale cases. Control or counterbalance ordering and learning where feasible. Record any added entry/learning burden, errors and existing alternatives that already suffice. A.9 will define proposed measures and thresholds; no fabricated baseline, test results or adoption forecasts.

Reject or narrow the claim if HPI supplies no incremental decision benefit, causes misleading interpretation, depends on unavailable current evidence, or cannot lawfully and safely supply the essential evidence. Do not treat a larger feature set as the solution without evidence and owner approval. No promise of lower purchase prices, prevention of regret, reduction of anxiety, objective valuation or replacement of professional advice. No data acquisition, recruitment, external contact, provider operation or implementation authorised by this task.
